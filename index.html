Food is wasted on one side of town while families go without on the other.

Businesses usually do want to give their surplus away. What they lack is a quick way to do it. Associations have the same problem from the other direction: they’d take the food, but they find out too late, or not at all.

GiveEat puts both sides on one screen. The business reports what’s left, the association sees it straight away, reserves it, and organises the pickup.

Our goal is to become the go-to way to redistribute food in Morocco, starting here in Safi.

Privacy policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data GiveEat handles and what your rights are. We follow Moroccan Law 09-08 on the protection of personal data and, where the law requires it, we notify the CNDP or ask for its authorisation before processing.

This website

The website has no accounts, no forms, no analytics and no advertising. We don’t ask you for any personal data here. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as your IP address and browser type) for security and to keep the site running.

If you choose “Allow Google Fonts” in the cookie banner, your browser loads our typefaces from Google, which receives your IP address. If you decline, the site uses fonts already on your device.

The GiveEat app and web app

To run the service, we process the details businesses and associations give us when they sign up and use it: names, contact details, the surplus-food listings they post, reservations and pickups, and notifications. We use this only to connect donors with associations, to show impact figures, and to support our users. We don’t sell personal data.

The Android app includes third-party software components: Google services for push notifications and a Meta (Facebook) software kit. These may receive technical information from your device, under their own privacy policies.

How long we keep data

We keep account data while your account is active. After that, we delete it, except what the law requires us to keep (for example, invoices).

Your rights, including deletion

You can ask us to give you access to your data, correct it, stop using it, or delete it. To request deletion, email us at giveeatstartup@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my data”, from the address linked to your account. We reply within 30 days. If you’re unhappy with our answer, you can complain to the CNDP (cndp.ma).

Children

GiveEat is for businesses and associations and is not meant for anyone under 18. We don’t knowingly collect data from children. If you think a child has given us data, contact us and we’ll delete it.

Emails

We don’t send marketing emails. If that ever changes, we’ll ask for your permission first and every email will have an unsubscribe link.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date above.

Terms of service

  • Food safety. Businesses must only offer food that is safe to eat and must follow food hygiene rules. Associations are responsible for how they store and hand out what they collect.

  • Our responsibility. We work to keep the service running, but we can’t promise it will never be interrupted, or that every offer will be collected.
  • Law. These terms are governed by Moroccan law.
